About Mr ZF

Quality Fabric, Honestly Sold

Mr ZF is a dedicated Khaddar fabric store. We focus on one thing and do it properly: Khaddar fabrics organized into four practical weights, from extra-warm winter fabric for the coldest days to lightweight weaves for the changing season.

Listings are managed through the owner dashboard. Please confirm current fabric details and stock availability when sending your order request. If you are unsure which weight suits your weather or your use, send a message — you will get a straight, honest answer before you order.

Ordering is simple: browse the collection, open the fabric you like and send an order request. The owner personally reviews every request and contacts you to confirm details and delivery.
