Help Center

Customer FAQ

What exactly is khaddar?

Khaddar (also spelled khadar) is a handloom-style cotton fabric woven with a slightly textured, breathable finish. It is the traditional winter fabric of Pakistan — warm without being synthetic, and it gets softer with every wash.

Which khaddar weight is right for my city?

For northern areas and severe cold (Murree, Quetta, Gilgit, Kashmir), choose Extreme Winter. For cold plains winters (Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar in December–January), choose Heavy Winter. For everyday mild winter, Regular Winter is ideal. For mild days and the transition into summer, choose Light & Transitional.

How do I wash and care for khaddar?

Hand wash or machine wash on gentle with cold water, using mild detergent. Avoid bleach. Dry in shade to keep the dye rich, and iron on medium heat while slightly damp. Khaddar softens beautifully after the first two washes.

Is the price per suit or per metre?

Each product page clearly shows the unit. Most products are priced per suit (shirt + trouser lengths listed under "Length"), and a few fabric items are priced per metre — the unit is written right next to the price.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

When you submit the checkout form, your order request is emailed to our team with a reference number. We then call or WhatsApp you on the number you provided to confirm stock, address and payment method. Nothing is charged before confirmation.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale quantities?

Yes — for bulk orders, gifting or wholesale pricing, contact us through the Contact form (choose "Bulk order") or message us on WhatsApp with the quantities you need.

Can I exchange a product if it doesn't suit me?

Unstitched items can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery if unused, unwashed and with original tags or packaging. Stitched or altered items cannot be exchanged. See our policy section below for details.
