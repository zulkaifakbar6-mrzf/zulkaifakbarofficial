About Mr ZF

Quality Fabric, Honestly Sold

Mr ZF is a dedicated Khaddar fabric store. We focus on one thing and do it properly: genuine Khaddar in four practical weights, from extra-warm winter fabric for the coldest days to lightweight weaves for the changing season.

Every listing on this site is managed personally by the owner, so the photos, details and availability you see here are always current. If you are unsure which weight suits your weather or your use, send a message — you will get a straight, honest answer before you order.

Ordering is simple: browse the collection, open the fabric you like and send an order request. The owner personally reviews every request and contacts you to confirm details and delivery.
